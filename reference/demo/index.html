Case study

Small sellers get onboarded to ONDC, but far fewer go live with a complete, compliant catalog.

Seller apps re-key catalogs by hand from Excel sheets, price-list photos and WhatsApp messages. Statutory fields such as MRP, net quantity and FSSAI licence are often missing. This demo shows an agentic onboarding flow that does the extraction, mapping and checking, while a human approves every step that carries legal or financial risk.

How the flow works

Stage 1 runs once per seller. Stage 2 runs for every catalog that seller adds. Nothing is published by default.

Stage 1 · Seller onboarding
S1 Seller intake agent→S2 Verification agent→G0 SNP reviewer approves seller
Stage 2 · Catalog onboarding, per seller
1 Source intake→2 Extraction→G1 Seller confirms items→3 Category→4 Smart mapping→G2 Reviewer approves risky mappings→5 Validation→G3 Seller signs off statutory fields→6 Catalog builder→G4 Pilot phase, then full catalog→7 Publish and monitor

Anything an agent cannot resolve goes to a fallback queue for the catalog ops reviewer, and comes back through G2.

Simulated agent

Agent steps replay pre-computed outputs for the sample data. No model is called in this demo, which keeps it free, fast and repeatable.

Real check

Deterministic checks run as real code in your browser: GSTIN checksum, PAN match, FSSAI expiry, IFSC format, GTIN check digit, MRP versus price, duplicates and required fields.

Human gate

You play both humans: the seller and the seller app's reviewer. Every approval, edit and rejection lands in the audit log.

Smart mapping, in one rule

Every proposed field mapping carries a confidence score and a one-line reason that quotes the seller's own values. Low-risk fields at 0.90 or above are auto-accepted. Prices, net quantity, FSSAI, packer and veg marks always go to a human. Anything below 0.60 goes to the fallback queue. The agent never invents a statutory value: it can only extract it, pre-fill it from the approved seller profile, or ask.

All sellers, people, GSTINs, PANs, FSSAI numbers, bank details and barcodes are fictional test data. ONDC attribute keys are modelled on the public retail v1.2 specs and simplified for the demo.
